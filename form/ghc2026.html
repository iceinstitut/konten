China–Indonesia Global Hybrid Classroom Program
Student Participation Guide

Welcome to the China–Indonesia Global Hybrid Classroom (GHC) Program! Through the support of the Global MOOC and Online Education Alliance, top Chinese universities are making real-time, hybrid courses available to Indonesian students. Using the Rain Classroom platform, Chinese university classrooms are "cloned" for online access, enabling real-time participation alongside students in China.

1 What You Can Expect

Real-time Class Experience: Join live lectures directly from top Chinese university classrooms.
Interactive Tools: Use Rain Classroom features like bullet comments, quizzes, and digital slide sync.
Flexible Learning: Access course slides, recordings (where available), and study materials anytime.
English Instruction: All courses offered in this program are taught in English.
Certificate & Credit Support: Earn an official certificate upon meeting attendance and assessment requirements.

2 Before Your Course Begins (Checklist)

1

Download Rain Classroom (xuetangX): Install the Rain Classroom app or access the Web version (www.rainclass.cn).

2

Create & Bind Account: Register your account and complete account verification using your registered email.

3

Join Your Cloned Class: Enter the Class Invitation Code provided by ICE Institute prior to the course start date.

4

Check Class Schedule & Time Differences: Pay attention to course times specified in Beijing Time (GMT+8) and convert to Western Indonesian Time (WIB / GMT+7).

5

Review Syllabus & Prerequisites: Ensure you understand the course expectations and possess the required basic knowledge.

3 During the Course Guidelines

Punctuality & Attendance

Log in to Rain Classroom at least 5 minutes before class starts. Attendance is automatically tracked by the system platform.

Active Engagement

Participate in pop-up quizzes, slide interactions, and discussion boards during live sessions.

Assignments & Quizzes

Submit homework, midterm projects, and final assessments punctually via Rain Classroom.

Academic Integrity

All work must be strictly your own. Plagiarism or unauthorized collusion will result in immediate disqualification.

4 Course Completion Requirements

Audit Track (Non-Credit)
  • Maintain at least 85% attendance rate throughout the semester.
  • Participate actively in class interactions and complete assigned survey forms.
Credit Track (Grade & SKS)
  • Maintain at least 85% attendance rate.
  • Achieve a cumulative final score of at least 70% (70/100) across all exams and coursework.
  • Valid active Passport Number registered in system.

5 Certificates and Academic Credit

Upon successfully meeting all course completion requirements, students will receive an official co-branded certificate issued by the host Chinese university and ICE Institute.

For students on the Credit Track, the certificate will include your final numeric grade and course credit value, which can be presented to your home university in Indonesia for academic credit transfer (SKS).

Sample Official GHC Certificate

6 Important Reminders

Time Zone Difference: Chinese Standard Time (CST/Beijing) is 1 hour ahead of WIB (Jakarta). Always adjust class times accordingly!

Notification Channel: Check your registered email and WhatsApp group daily for urgent announcements.

Helpdesk: For technical issues with Rain Classroom, contact team in WhatsApp Group
